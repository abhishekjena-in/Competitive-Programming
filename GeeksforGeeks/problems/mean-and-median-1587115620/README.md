### 📝 Problem Statement
Given an array of positive integer arr[]. Find the mean and median of the array.  Where mean is the average value of the given array, median is the average of the two middle elements.

Note: Return the floor value of mean and median.

Constraints:1 ≤ arr.size() ≤ 1051 ≤ arr[i] ≤ 104
